Research & Experience

Omni-modal evaluation at NAVER Cloud

Evaluating multiple models, checking evaluation consistency, and developing OmniNSP, with supporting contributions to text evaluation in OmniEvaluator.

Continued pretraining & long-context learning

Hands-on continued pretraining of 4B and 8B language models on over one billion tokens, using H100 multi-node training. The research explores multilingual long-context learning and cross-lingual transfer.

Memory for web agents

Studying how web agents reuse past experience through evaluation and analysis of the relationship between memory and agent behavior.

Confidence-based adaptive reasoning

Implementing and evaluating confidence-based reasoning control to examine the relationship between accuracy and generated-token cost.

In-context learning

Analyzing demonstration and label roles through controlled comparisons, and studying model-generated examples for in-context learning.

Reliable generation

Studying responses to ambiguous questions and noisy retrieved context through model alignment and adaptive decoding.
