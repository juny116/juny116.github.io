Research & Experience

Omni-modal evaluation at NAVER Cloud

Benchmark design and evaluation-framework contributions, including consistency checks, selected implementation, OmniNSP development, and supporting text-evaluation work in OmniEvaluator.

Continued pretraining & long-context learning

Hands-on continued pretraining of 4B and 8B language models on over one billion tokens, using H100 multi-node training. The research studies cross-language retrieval transfer and within-document language switching.

Memory for web agents

Separating initial guidance from continued access to a fixed experience bank, and studying their effects on web-agent behavior.

Confidence-based adaptive reasoning

Implementing and evaluating confidence-based reasoning control to examine the relationship between accuracy and generated-token cost.

In-context learning

Analyzing demonstration and label roles through controlled comparisons, and studying model-generated examples for in-context learning.

Reliable generation

Studying responses to ambiguous questions and noisy retrieved context through model alignment and adaptive decoding.
