Continued pretraining & long-context learning

Continued pretraining of language models, combining hands-on training experience with research on multilingual long-context learning.

Work: OngoingPaper: Under review

Training experience

My hands-on continued pretraining experience includes 4B and 8B language models, training on over one billion tokens, and H100 multi-node training.

Research question

How does monolingual continued pretraining transfer to retrieval in another language, and how does the language composition of a long document affect that transfer?

Approach

The study examines cross-language retrieval after monolingual continued pretraining and explores switching languages within a document as a data-construction approach.

Observations & open questions

We observe cross-language transfer in retrieval and continue to examine its scope and the role of within-document language switching. Long-context learning is a potential foundation for agents; this study has not evaluated agentic tasks.
