Continued pretraining & long-context learning

Continued pretraining of language models, combining hands-on training experience with research on multilingual long-context learning.

Work: OngoingPaper: Under review

Training experience

My hands-on continued pretraining experience includes 4B and 8B language models, training on over one billion tokens, and H100 multi-node training.

Research question

This research studies multilingual long-context mid-training: continued pretraining that develops a model’s ability to use longer inputs across languages. I am interested in the relationship between how training data is composed and how a model uses information spread throughout a long context.

Approach

I examine these questions through data construction and evaluation, asking how long-context learning in one language relates to performance in others. The aim is to understand how models learn to use information across a long input, beyond simply accepting more tokens.

Open questions

The study explores how to assess long-context use across languages and how data composition relates to the capabilities being measured. These remain research questions, rather than established conclusions about which training choices work best.

The manuscript is under review. Follow-up experiments and revisions continue as part of the study.
