Confidence-based adaptive reasoning

Implementing and evaluating confidence-based reasoning control, examining accuracy alongside generated-token cost.

Paper: Published

Research question

Long reasoning traces can involve both stopping too early and continuing beyond what is useful. This work studies self-assessed confidence as a signal for choosing when to stop.

Approach

The method asks a model to assess its confidence at intermediate reasoning steps, then uses that signal to decide whether to continue. See the paper for the evaluation setup and results.
