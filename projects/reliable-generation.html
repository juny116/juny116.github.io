Reliable generation

Studying responses to ambiguous questions and noisy retrieved context through alignment and adaptive decoding.

Paper: Published

Research question

These studies address uncertainty in a question and unreliability in its retrieved evidence.

Approach

Aligning Language Models to Explicitly Handle Ambiguity uses perceived ambiguity to guide model alignment. Adaptive Contrastive Decoding adjusts the influence of retrieved context during generation when that context may be noisy.

Observations

In the evaluated question-answering settings, the first study improves ambiguity handling while retaining clear-question performance. The second improves over evaluated baselines, particularly with noisy retrieval. These findings apply to the tested tasks and models.
