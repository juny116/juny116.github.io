In-context learning

Analyzing demonstration labels through controlled comparisons and studying model-generated examples for in-context learning.

Paper: Published

Research question

These studies examine what information demonstrations provide and how useful examples can be constructed.

Approach

Ground-Truth Labels Matter analyzes correct input–label mappings through controlled comparisons. Self-Generated In-Context Learning develops and evaluates demonstrations generated by a language model, conditioned on the test input and candidate class.

Observations

GTLM finds that label correctness matters differently across experimental configurations. SG-ICL improves over zero-shot learning on the evaluated text-classification tasks and shows lower variance than randomly selected training demonstrations. The two studies address label information and example construction, respectively.
